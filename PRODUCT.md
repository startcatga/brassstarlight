# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

Flutter 3.47 (Dart), Android phones; web build used only for previews.

## Users and job

Players who want a calm, short puzzle session (1–3 minutes per night), often
in the evening. The job: relax while doing something that feels skilful —
finding and drawing constellations — and watching a personal star atlas fill.

## What makes it different

The puzzle grows from guided tracing to pattern-finding under rotation to
one-stroke figure drawing, all inside one consistent fiction: an apprentice
charting the sky from a rooftop observatory.

## Constraints

- Fully offline. No ads, purchases, accounts, analytics or permissions.
- No timers or lives; restart is free; hints are earned.
- Accessibility: never colour alone, 48dp targets, reduce-motion setting.
- Publisher details never appear in the app.

## Voice

Quiet, ceremonial, theme-native ("Trace Tonight's Sky", "Leave the Deck",
"Constellation Sealed"). No generic "Play/Settings".
